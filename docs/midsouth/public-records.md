# Mid-South public-records brief

**Research date:** 2026-08-14  
**Scope:** Olive Branch, MS; Memphis, TN; DeSoto County, MS (Southaven, Horn Lake, Hernando, county sheriff).  
**This document:** inventory and citations only.

Checked 2026-08-15: cited government and news URLs below returned HTTP 200 except WREG and the Flock-hosted Shelby County transparency portal (both 403 to an automated fetch; pages are still the public citations). No extra contract totals or camera counts were added beyond the sources named here.

## What this is not

- Not an official intersection or pole list. None of the core cities published one.
- Not proof that a mapped OSM pin is a specific agency camera.
- Not a Flock vendor API, live plate-read feed, or field survey.
- DeFlock is OSM-backed (same `surveillance:type=ALPR` nodes), not an independent coordinate database. License: [OpenStreetMap ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

**Confirmed** here means only that an *agency* appears in a public Flock / ALPR record. It does not mean a coordinate was field-verified.

## Olive Branch, MS

### 2022 Utility / Coreforce entranceway cameras

In 2022 the city accepted a Utility Associates (Coreforce) plan for **24** cameras at unnamed city entranceways, about **$297k / 5 years**. Board of Aldermen minutes for **2022-08-02** accepted Utility Associates. News coverage and the minutes do not name the intersections.

- [DeSoto County News — license plate reader cameras coming to Olive Branch](https://desotocountynews.com/desoto-county-news/license-plate-reader-cameras-coming-to-olive-branch/)
- [DeSoto Times — Olive Branch to install license plate reading cameras at entrance ways](https://www.desototimes.com/news/olive-branch-to-install-license-plate-reading-cameras-at-entrance-ways/article_f2e4e8cc-1348-11ed-b061-eff3eb3e61c6.html)
- [Olive Branch Board of Aldermen minutes — 2022-08-02](https://obms.us/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Minutes/_08022022-164)

The geographic reconstruction of those unnamed crossings is in [olive-branch-entrances.md](olive-branch-entrances.md). A mapped pin at an entrance is **not** a confirmed 2022 Utility camera.

### December 2024 Flock Group SaaS

On **2024-12-17**, consent item **16** approved Flock Group SaaS for LPR equipment. The minutes and agenda do not include a pin list or a camera count.

- [Minutes — 2024-12-17](https://www.obms.us/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Minutes/_12172024-415)
- [Agenda — 2024-12-17](https://www.obms.us/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_12172024-415)

### Mississippi DFA RFP (no published award)

Mississippi DFA bid **RFx 3170029485** closed with **no published award**.

- [MS DFA Bid Details 37418](https://www.ms.gov/dfa/contract_bid_search/Bid/Details/37418)

## Mapped data vs. official lists

| Source | What it is |
|---|---|
| OpenStreetMap `surveillance:type=ALPR` | Volunteer-mapped nodes. Incomplete by nature. ODbL. |
| DeFlock | Same OSM nodes, rendered. Not a second coordinate database. |
| Atlas of Surveillance | Agency / vendor rows from open-source research. Can lag contracts (Olive Branch vendor) or mis-file state (Horn Lake). |
| City / county minutes | Hardware and SaaS approvals. **No official intersection list** for Olive Branch, Southaven, Horn Lake, Hernando, or Memphis in the sources above. |

The in-app map queries Overpass live. This brief does not replace that fetch and does not write to OSM.
